**Registration** takes place at the venue on race day, until 15 minutes before the start. The fee is EUR 5.00 per race. An additional EUR 1.00 is charged at the first registration of the season for runners who were not ranked in the previous season.

**Wearing your bib** is mandatory throughout the race. Pin it to your chest and keep the sponsor’s name visible.

Your first participation determines your **category** for the season. Your bib number and registered **club** remain valid throughout the season. Organisers collect bibs at the end of each race. Remember your number: you will receive the same number at subsequent races. Lists will be available to help you find it.

You do not need to belong to a **club** to take part.

## Age requirements

Participants must be at least 10 years old. For distances over 12 km, runners must be 12 on race day. For distances over 15 km, the minimum age is 16.

Timing ends after a duration calculated at 8 minutes per kilometre (7.5 km/h). Runners exceeding this limit may finish safely but will not be ranked and will receive no challenge points.

**Categories** apply from 1 January, based on the year of birth.

ED (Young women) ages 10–19 in the year
DA (Women) ages 20–39 in the year
A1 (Women masters 1) ages 40–49 in the year
A2 (Women masters 2) ages 50–59 in the year
A3 (Women masters 3) ages 60–69 in the year
A4 (Women masters 4) age 70 and above in the year
EH (Young men) ages 10–19 in the year
SE (Senior men) ages 20–39 in the year
V1 (Men masters 1) ages 40–49 in the year
V2 (Men masters 2) ages 50–59 in the year
V3 (Men masters 3) ages 60–69 in the year
V4 (Men masters 4) ages 70–79 in the year
V5 (Men masters 5) age 80 and above in the year

Runners enter the overall standings after participating in at least 12 races during the season.

The twelve best results count towards the final standings.

## Points formula

Points = (D x D)/4 x 15 + (NP - P) / NP x 4000 + (D x 1000) / TS x 1000 + (Vref - Vp25) x 1000

D = distance in kilometres
NP = number of participants
P = finishing position
D x 1000 = distance in metres
TS = finishing time in seconds
Vref = reference runner’s speed (4 min 23 sec/km), in m/s
Vp25 = speed of the last runner in the first quarter of the field, in m/s

This formula is the intellectual property of ASBL ACRHO. Use outside the challenge requires a written request to its governing body.

A bonus of 1,000 points is awarded for every race after the twelfth participation, up to the twenty-fifth (maximum 13,000 points).

**Awards ceremony:** a cup or trophy is awarded to the top three challenge finishers in each category listed above.

The awards ceremony takes place around mid-December, one to two weeks after the last race.
